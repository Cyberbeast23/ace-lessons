Ace Cantonese Pronunciation Audio
=================================

For Ace (beginner). Hong Kong Cantonese, Traditional Chinese characters.
TTS voice (Cantonese): zh-HK-HiuMaanNeural
TTS voice (English in ALL-words.mp3): en-HK-YanNeural
Rate: slowed (~-20%) for clear listening practice.

Sources: Ace lessons 2026-09-29 (Greetings) + 2026-09-30 (Family Members) + 2026-10-01 (Food) + 2026-10-02 (School).
Numbers 1-10 are already known and not included here.

How to practise:
  1. Play one clip, look at the Chinese characters, say it out loud three times.
  2. Or play ALL-words.mp3: you hear English, then the Cantonese word.
