Today, while your other lessons follow food on its journey through the digestive system, you'll learn words for digestion and eating: digest, stomach, small intestine, teeth, saliva, swallow, bite and full. You already know 口 (hau2, mouth), 牙 (ngaa4, tooth), 肚餓 (tou5 ngo6, hungry), 好食 (hou2 sik6, delicious) and 健康 (gin6 hong1, healthy), so you can talk about a whole meal from first bite to last! We use Traditional Chinese characters and Jyutping with tone numbers, the Hong Kong way.

How to read Jyutping (sounds):
  • Letters show the sound. Numbers show the tone (the musical pitch): 1 high, 2 rising, 3 mid, 4 low/falling, 5 low rising, 6 low.
  • Example: 吞 tan1 is tone 1, high and flat, but 胃 wai6 is tone 6, low and level. 咬 ngaau5 is tone 5, low and rising, and 飽 baau2 is tone 2, rising from the middle. Listen for the difference!
  • Listening/speaking tip: Say each word out loud three times while looking at the characters. Tap Hear model, then Record and compare!

Digestion & eating tips for Ace:
  • 消化 siu1 faa3 = to digest. 化 means "to change," the same 化 as in 化石 (faa3 sek6, fossil), so digestion is food that has been changed!
  • 小腸 siu2 coeng4 = small intestine (小 small + 腸 intestine). Can you guess 大腸? It's daai6 coeng4, the large intestine!
  • 牙齒 ngaa4 ci2 = teeth. You already know 牙 (ngaa4). Bonus: 刷牙 caat3 ngaa4 = brush your teeth.
  • 口水 hau2 seoi2 = saliva (mouth + water). 流口水 lau4 hau2 seoi2 = your mouth is watering!
  • 食飽 sik6 baau2 = eaten until full. 我食飽喇！ (ngo5 sik6 baau2 laa3!) is the polite way to say "I'm full!" at a Hong Kong dinner table. Its opposite is 肚餓 (tou5 ngo6, hungry).
