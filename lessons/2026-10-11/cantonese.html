🗣️ Cantonese Week 13: Trains & Travel 火車 (Hong Kong)

Ace, you already know numbers up to 100, plus greetings, family, food, school, body parts, colours, sky & space words, what to say at the doctor's, nature words, question words, and music words. Today, while your other lessons explore the building of the Canadian Pacific Railway, you'll learn train and travel words: train, station, ride, go, near, Canada, railway, and worker. Here's something special: most of the Chinese workers who helped build the railway through BC came from Guangdong (廣東 gwong2 dung1) in southern China, and they spoke Cantonese, the same language you're learning! We use Traditional Chinese characters and Jyutping with tone numbers, the Hong Kong way.

How to read Jyutping (sounds):
  • Letters show the sound. Numbers show the tone (the musical pitch): 1 high, 2 rising, 3 mid, 4 low/falling, 5 low rising, 6 low.
  • Example: ce1 in 火車 (train) — say ce with tone 1 (high and level), like a long train whistle.
  • Listening/speaking tip: Say each word out loud three times while looking at the characters. Tap Hear model, then Record and compare!

Train tips for Ace:
  • 火車 fo2 ce1 is literally "fire vehicle," because the first trains were steam engines powered by fire. You learned 火 fo2 (fire) on nature day!
  • 車 ce1 means vehicle. It's in 火車 (train) and 車站 ce1 zaam6 (station: literally "vehicle stop").
  • 鐵路 tit3 lou6 is literally "iron road." What a great name for a railway!
  • Use 坐 co5 (sit) to say you ride something: 坐火車 = take the train.
  • 近 kan5 (near) is the opposite of 遠 jyun5 (far). Remember 好遠 from sky & space day?
  • Combine with question words: 你去邊度？ nei5 heoi3 bin1 dou6? = Where are you going? 🚂
