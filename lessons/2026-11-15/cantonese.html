Today, while your other lessons follow the life story of the stars, you'll learn words for stargazing and the universe: universe, telescope, constellation, the Milky Way, shooting star, make a wish, explode and black hole. You already know 星星 (sing1 sing1, star), 夜空 (je6 hung1, night sky), 閃 (sim2, to twinkle), 發光 (faat3 gwong1, to glow) and 太空人 (taai3 hung1 jan4, astronaut), so you can go stargazing in Cantonese! We use Traditional Chinese characters and Jyutping with tone numbers, the Hong Kong way.

How to read Jyutping (sounds):
  • Letters show the sound. Numbers show the tone (the musical pitch): 1 high, 2 rising, 3 mid, 4 low/falling, 5 low rising, 6 low.
  • Today's words use all six tones! 星 sing1 is tone 1 (high and flat), 許 heoi2 is tone 2 (rising), 爆 baau3 is tone 3 (middle), 河 ho4 is tone 4 (low and falling), 宇 jyu5 is tone 5 (low rising), and 宙 zau6 is tone 6 (low and level). Listen for the difference!
  • Listening/speaking tip: Say each word out loud three times while looking at the characters. Tap Hear model, then Record and compare!

Stargazing tips for Ace:
  • 望遠鏡 mong6 jyun5 geng3 = telescope. 望 = look, 遠 = far, 鏡 = lens, so a telescope is a "look-far lens"! You know 眼鏡 (ngaan5 geng2, glasses). Notice that 鏡 changes to tone 2 in 眼鏡.
  • 銀河 ngan4 ho4 = the Milky Way. 銀 = silver and 河 = river (you know 河 ho4!). In Chinese, our galaxy is a "silver river" across the sky, not a road of milk.
  • 流星 lau4 sing1 = shooting star. 流 = to flow and 星 = star: a star that flows across the sky! (Really, it's a tiny space rock burning up.)
  • 黑洞 hak1 dung6 = black hole. You know 黑色 (hak1 sik1, black), and 洞 = hole. Easy!
  • 星座 sing1 zo6 = constellation. People in Hong Kong also use it for star signs: 你係咩星座？ (nei5 hai6 me1 sing1 zo6? What's your star sign?)
