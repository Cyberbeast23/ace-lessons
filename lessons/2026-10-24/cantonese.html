Ace, you already know numbers up to 100, plus greetings, family, food, school, body parts, colours, sky & space words, what to say at the doctor's, nature words, question words, music words, trains & travel, brain & sleep words, bees & gardens, electricity & lights, land, people & respect, ice, snow & giant animals, community, rules & voting, weather & storms, germ-fighter words, ancient China words, flight words, gold rush words, and kitchen & flavour words. Today, while your other lessons explore how cartoons move, you'll learn cartoon and movie words: cartoon, animation, to draw, cartoon character, movie, to watch, to laugh, and slow. You already know 好 (hou2), 我 (ngo5), 筆 (bat1, pen) and 故事 (gu3 si6, story), so listen for them in today's sentences. We use Traditional Chinese characters and Jyutping with tone numbers, the Hong Kong way.

How to read Jyutping (sounds):
  • Letters show the sound. Numbers show the tone (the musical pitch): 1 high, 2 rising, 3 mid, 4 low/falling, 5 low rising, 6 low.
  • Example: 畫畫 waak6 waa2 (to draw) uses the same character twice with two different sounds! The first 畫 is the action, waak6, tone 6 (low and level). The second 畫 is the picture, waa2, tone 2 (rising, like asking a question). Compare with 笑 siu3, tone 3 (mid and level), and 慢 maan6, tone 6, low and level, nice and slow!
  • Listening/speaking tip: Say each word out loud three times while looking at the characters. Tap Hear model, then Record and compare!

Cartoon tips for Ace:
  • 卡通 kaa1 tung1 sounds a lot like the English word "cartoon," because Cantonese borrowed it! Both syllables are tone 1, high and flat.
  • 動畫 dung6 waa2 means animation: 動 = moving, 畫 = picture. "Moving pictures," exactly what you learned in today's reading! Some speakers say dung6 waak6; both are accepted in the typing quiz.
  • 畫畫 waak6 waa2 = to draw (literally "draw a picture"). 一幅畫 (jat1 fuk1 waa2) = a picture.
  • 公仔 gung1 zai2 = a cartoon character, a doll, or a figure. 畫公仔 (waak6 gung1 zai2) = to draw cartoon characters.
  • 電影 din6 jing2 = movie: 電 (electric, you learned it on Oct. 14!) + 影 (shadow or image), "electric shadows."
  • 睇 tai2 = to watch or look at. 睇卡通 = watch cartoons; 睇電影 = watch a movie.
  • 笑 siu3 = to laugh or smile, and 好笑 (hou2 siu3) = funny. 慢 maan6 = slow, perfect for slow motion! A cartoon fan's sentence: 我鍾意睇卡通同埋畫畫！ (ngo5 zung1 ji3 tai2 kaa1 tung1 tung4 maai4 waak6 waa2!) = I like watching cartoons and drawing! 🎬
