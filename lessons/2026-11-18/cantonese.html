You already know 冰 (bing1, ice), 比賽 (bei2 coi3, a match), 贏 (jeng4, win), 輸 (syu1, lose), 頭盔 (tau4 kwai1, helmet) and 加油 (gaa1 jau2, Go! You can do it!), so you can cheer on your own team in Cantonese! We use Traditional Chinese characters and Jyutping with tone numbers, the Hong Kong way.

How to read Jyutping (sounds):
  • Letters show the sound. Numbers show the tone (the musical pitch): 1 high, 2 rising, 3 mid, 4 low/falling, 5 low rising, 6 low.
  • Today's words use all six tones! 冰 bing1 is tone 1 (high and flat), 守 sau2 is tone 2 (rising), 教 gaau3 is tone 3 (middle), 球 kau4 is tone 4 (low and falling), 友 jau5 is tone 5 (low rising), and 練 lin6 is tone 6 (low and level). Listen for the difference!
  • Listening/speaking tip: Say each word out loud three times while looking at the characters. Tap Hear model, then Record and compare!

Hockey tips for Ace:
  • 冰球 bing1 kau4 = ice hockey, word for word "ice ball." 球 kau4 means ball (or any game played with one), and it shows up in four of today's words! You already met it inside 地球 (dei6 kau4), "Earth," the ball we live on.
  • 隊友 deoi6 jau5 = teammate: 隊 = team + 友 = friend. A teammate is a "team friend"!
  • 教練 gaau3 lin6 = coach: 教 = to teach + 練 = to practise. A coach teaches you how to practise.
  • 守門員 sau2 mun4 jyun4 = goalie: 守 = to guard + 門 = door/gate (the net!) + 員 = a person with a job. You've seen 員 before in 運動員 (wan6 dung6 jyun4, athlete).
  • 傳球 cyun4 kau4 = to pass and 入球 jap6 kau4 = to score a goal (word for word "ball goes in"). 分數 fan1 sou3 = the score, and 球迷 kau4 mai4 = fans: 迷 means someone who is crazy about something. Scores are said with 比 bei2: 三比二 = 3 to 2.
