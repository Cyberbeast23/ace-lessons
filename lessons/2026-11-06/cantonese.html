We use Traditional Chinese characters and Jyutping with tone numbers, the Hong Kong way.

How to read Jyutping (sounds):
  • Letters show the sound. Numbers show the tone (the musical pitch): 1 high, 2 rising, 3 mid, 4 low/falling, 5 low rising, 6 low.
  • Example: 骨 gwat1 (bone) is tone 1, high and flat, but 掘 gwat6 (to dig) is tone 6, low. Same starting sound, different tone — and both are perfect fossil-hunter words!
  • Listening/speaking tip: Say each word out loud three times while looking at the characters. Tap Hear model, then Record and compare!

Dinosaur tips for Ace:
  • 恐龍 hung2 lung4 = dinosaur, literally "terrible dragon." Add 蛋 (daan2, egg) and you get 恐龍蛋, dinosaur egg!
  • 化石 faa3 sek6 = fossil, literally "changed stone" — which is exactly what mineralization does.
  • 博物館 bok3 mat6 gun2 = museum. 館 (gun2) means a hall or building for a purpose.
  • 骨頭 gwat1 tau4 = bone. 掘 gwat6 = to dig — listen for the tone change from 1 to 6!
  • 巨大 geoi6 daai6 = huge / gigantic. 兇惡 hung1 ngok6 = fierce / ferocious. Together: a huge fierce dinosaur!
