Today, while your other lessons explore how your eyes see, you'll learn words for eyes and seeing: glasses, sunglasses, eye doctor, nearsighted, can't see clearly, blink, tears and sleepy. You already know 眼 (ngaan5, eye), 睇 (tai2, look) and 醫生 (ji1 sang1, doctor), so lots of today's words will feel familiar! We use Traditional Chinese characters and Jyutping with tone numbers, the Hong Kong way.

How to read Jyutping (sounds):
  • Letters show the sound. Numbers show the tone (the musical pitch): 1 high, 2 rising, 3 mid, 4 low/falling, 5 low rising, 6 low.
  • Example: 瞓 fan3 (sleep) is tone 3, in the middle, but 分 fan1 (minute) is tone 1, high and flat. Same sound, different tone: 眼瞓 means sleepy, but 一分鐘 is one minute!
  • Listening/speaking tip: Say each word out loud three times while looking at the characters. Tap Hear model, then Record and compare!

Eye tips for Ace:
  • 眼 ngaan5 = eye, and it's hiding in six of today's words! Look for it in 眼鏡, 太陽眼鏡, 眼科醫生, 眨眼, 眼淚 and 眼瞓.
  • 眼鏡 ngaan5 geng2 = glasses, literally "eye lenses." Add 太陽 (taai3 joeng4, sun) in front and you get 太陽眼鏡, sunglasses! To wear glasses is 戴眼鏡 (daai3 ngaan5 geng2), the same 戴 as wearing a helmet.
  • 眼科醫生 ngaan5 fo1 ji1 sang1 = eye doctor. 科 (fo1) means a branch of study, so 眼科 is "the eye department."
  • 近視 kan5 si6 = nearsighted, literally "near-seeing": you see near things well, but far things look blurry.
  • 睇唔清楚 tai2 m4 cing1 co2 = can't see clearly. 唔 (m4) means "not," so 睇清楚 = see clearly, and 睇唔清楚 = can't see clearly.
  • 眼瞓 ngaan5 fan3 = sleepy: your eyes want to 瞓 (fan3, sleep). Very Hong Kong!
