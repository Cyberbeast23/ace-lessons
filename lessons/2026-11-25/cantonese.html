You already know 乾淨 (gon1 zeng6, clean) and 地球 (dei6 kau4, the Earth), so now you can talk about keeping the Earth clean! We use Traditional Chinese characters and Jyutping with tone numbers, the Hong Kong way.

How to read Jyutping (sounds):
  • Letters show the sound. Numbers show the tone (the musical pitch): 1 high, 2 rising, 3 mid, 4 low/falling, 5 low rising, 6 low.
  • Today's words use five of the six tones. 收 sau1 is tone 1 (high and flat), 保 bou2 is tone 2 (rising), 費 fai3 is tone 3 (middle and flat), 回 wui4 is tone 4 (low and falling), and 垃 laap6 is tone 6 (low and flat). Listen for a pair that shares a sound: 堆填區 deoi1 tin4 keoi1 (landfill) and 堆肥 deoi1 fei4 (compost) both start with 堆 deoi1, "a pile"!
  • Listening/speaking tip: Say each word out loud three times while looking at the characters. Tap Hear model, then Record and compare!

Garbage & recycling tips for Ace:
  • 垃圾 laap6 saap3 = garbage. Add 桶 (tung2, bucket) to get 垃圾桶 (laap6 saap3 tung2), a garbage can! 唔好亂掉垃圾！ = Don't litter!
  • 回收 wui4 sau1 = to recycle: 回 (to return, go back) + 收 (to collect). Things "go back to be collected" and made into something new, just like the English re-cycle.
  • 膠樽 gaau1 zeon1 = plastic bottle: 膠 (plastic, glue) + 樽 (bottle). This is a very Hong Kong word! In Mandarin you'd hear a different word, but in Hong Kong everyone says 膠樽.
  • 堆填區 deoi1 tin4 keoi1 = landfill: 堆 (pile) + 填 (to fill in) + 區 (area), "the area you fill in with piles." 堆肥 deoi1 fei4 = compost: 堆 (pile) + 肥 (fertilizer, fat), a "fertilizer pile."
  • 分類 fan1 leoi6 = to sort into types: 分 (to divide) + 類 (kind, type). 環保 waan4 bou2 = eco-friendly: short for 環境保護, "protecting the environment." 浪費 long6 fai3 = to waste: 唔好浪費！ = Don't waste!
