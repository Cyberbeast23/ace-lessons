🧮 Math Week 1: Medical Math Challenge

Doctors and nurses use math all day: counting heartbeats, working out medicine doses, and spotting patterns. This week, Ace, you'll train like a medical team with multi-step problems, fractions and decimals, big-number multiplication and division, and patterns and equations.

Part 1: Learn the Moves

The 4-Step Problem Solver (like a doctor's check-up):
  1. Read: What is the question actually asking? Underline the numbers and units.
  2. Plan: Which operations do I need, and in what order?
  3. Solve: Do one step at a time and write each step down.
  4. Check: Is my answer reasonable? Did I answer the question that was asked?

Worked Example 1: Multi-step problem (rates)

A puppy's heart beats 120 times per minute. How many times does it beat in a quarter of an hour?

Step 1: A quarter of an hour = 60 ÷ 4 = 15 minutes.

Step 2: 120 beats × 15 minutes = 1800 beats.

Check: 120 × 10 = 1200 and 120 × 5 = 600, and 1200 + 600 = 1800 ✔

Worked Example 2: Adding fractions with different denominators

A patient drinks 2/3 L of juice and 1/4 L of water. How much altogether?

Step 1: Find a common denominator. Multiples of 3 and 4 meet at 12.

Step 2: 2/3 = 8/12 and 1/4 = 3/12.

Step 3: 8/12 + 3/12 = 11/12 L.

To subtract mixed numbers, you may need to regroup: 3 1/5 − 1 3/5 → 2 6/5 − 1 3/5 = 1 3/5.

Worked Example 3: Ratios and doses

A (pretend) vitamin syrup is given at 3 mL for every 5 kg of body mass. How much for a 40 kg person?

Step 1: How many groups of 5 kg are in 40 kg? 40 ÷ 5 = 8 groups.

Step 2: 8 groups × 3 mL = 24 mL.

Real medicine safety: only a doctor, pharmacist, or parent decides real doses!

Worked Example 4: Long division check

6 552 ÷ 18 = ?

18 goes into 65 three times (54), remainder 11. Bring down 5 → 115.

18 goes into 115 six times (108), remainder 7. Bring down 2 → 72.

18 goes into 72 four times (72), remainder 0. Answer: 364.

Check by multiplying: 364 × 18 = 6552 ✔

Worked Example 5: Patterns and equations

Pattern: 5, 8, 11, 14, … The rule is "start at 5, add 3". Term number n is 3n + 2.

n1234
3n + 2581114

So the 10th term is 3 × 10 + 2 = 32.

Equation: 2x + 5 = 17. Undo the steps backwards: subtract 5 → 2x = 12, divide by 2 → x = 6. Check: 2 × 6 + 5 = 17 ✔

Order of operations (BEDMAS): Brackets, Exponents, Division and Multiplication (left to right), Addition and Subtraction (left to right).
