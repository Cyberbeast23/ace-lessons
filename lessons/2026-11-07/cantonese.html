We use Traditional Chinese characters and Jyutping with tone numbers, the Hong Kong way.

How to read Jyutping (sounds):
  • Letters show the sound. Numbers show the tone (the musical pitch): 1 high, 2 rising, 3 mid, 4 low/falling, 5 low rising, 6 low.
  • Example: 北 bak1 (north) is tone 1, high and flat, but 極 gik6 (pole / extreme) is tone 6, low. Put them together for 北極 — the North Pole!
  • Listening/speaking tip: Say each word out loud three times while looking at the characters. Tap Hear model, then Record and compare!

Night-sky tips for Ace:
  • 北極 bak1 gik6 = North Pole / Arctic. 南極 naam4 gik6 = South Pole / Antarctic. Same 極 (gik6)!
  • 發光 faat3 gwong1 = to glow / give off light. Perfect for talking about the aurora!
  • 閃 sim2 = to flash / twinkle. Stars 閃; auroras can 閃 too.
  • 夜空 je6 hung1 = night sky. You already know 夜晚 (night).
  • 漂亮 piu3 loeng2 = beautiful. 特別 dak6 bit6 = special. 神奇 san4 kei4 = amazing / magical. Tonight's aurora is 特別同神奇！
