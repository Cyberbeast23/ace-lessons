Today, while your other lessons explore the engineering of the bicycle, you'll learn words for bikes and riding: bicycle, ride a bike, helmet, wheel, brake, uphill, downhill and fall down. You already know 好快 (hou2 faai3, very fast), 慢 (maan6, slow) and 危險 (ngai4 him2, dangerous), so you can talk about a bike ride right away! We use Traditional Chinese characters and Jyutping with tone numbers, the Hong Kong way.

How to read Jyutping (sounds):
  • Letters show the sound. Numbers show the tone (the musical pitch): 1 high, 2 rising, 3 mid, 4 low/falling, 5 low rising, 6 low.
  • Example: 車 ce1 (vehicle) is tone 1, high and flat, but 斜 ce3 (slope) is tone 3, in the middle. Same sound, different tone: 單車 is a bike, but 上斜 is going uphill!
  • Listening/speaking tip: Say each word out loud three times while looking at the characters. Tap Hear model, then Record and compare!

Bike tips for Ace:
  • 單車 daan1 ce1 = bicycle, literally "single vehicle." 車 (ce1) is in lots of words you know: 火車 (fo2 ce1, train) and 白車 (baak6 ce1, ambulance). Today it shows up in 單車, 車轆 and 剎車!
  • 踩單車 caai2 daan1 ce1 = to ride a bike. 踩 (caai2) means "to step on" or "to pedal," and it has 足 (foot) on the left. Feet push the pedals!
  • 頭盔 tau4 kwai1 = helmet. You already know 頭 (tau4, head), so a helmet is a "head" cover. 戴頭盔 (daai3 tau4 kwai1) = wear a helmet.
  • 車轆 ce1 luk1 = wheel. 轆 (luk1) also means "to roll," so a wheel is the "rolling" part of a vehicle.
  • 上斜 soeng5 ce3 = go uphill and 落斜 lok6 ce3 = go downhill. 上 = up, 落 = down, 斜 = slope. You know 落雨 (lok6 jyu5, rain falls down) too!
