🗣️ Cantonese Week 22: Germs, Vaccines & Staying Healthy 健康 (Hong Kong)

Ace, you already know numbers up to 100, plus greetings, family, food, school, body parts, colours, sky & space words, what to say at the doctor's, nature words, question words, music words, trains & travel, brain & sleep words, bees & gardens, electricity & lights, land, people & respect, ice, snow & giant animals, community, rules & voting, and weather & storms. Today, while your other lessons explore the immune system, you'll learn germ-fighter words: germs, virus, vaccine, to get a shot, to cough, soap, face mask, and healthy. Bonus: the immune system is 免疫系統 (min5 jik6 hai6 tung2)! We use Traditional Chinese characters and Jyutping with tone numbers, the Hong Kong way.

How to read Jyutping (sounds):
  • Letters show the sound. Numbers show the tone (the musical pitch): 1 high, 2 rising, 3 mid, 4 low/falling, 5 low rising, 6 low.
  • Example: kat1 in 咳 (to cough) — say kat with tone 1 (high and flat) and stop it short, like a quick little cough: kat!
  • Listening/speaking tip: Say each word out loud three times while looking at the characters. Tap Hear model, then Record and compare!

Germ-fighter tips for Ace:
  • 細菌 sai3 kwan2 means germs or bacteria. 細 (sai3) means small and 菌 (kwan2) means germ, so 細菌 are tiny germs! 細菌好細 = germs are very small.
  • 病毒 beng6 duk6 means virus. 病 (beng6) = sickness and 毒 (duk6) = poison, so a virus is a "sickness poison."
  • 疫苗 jik6 miu4 means vaccine. 苗 (miu4) means a young sprout, like a tiny seedling that grows your protection! The same 疫 (jik6) is in 免疫系統 (min5 jik6 hai6 tung2), the immune system.
  • 打針 daa2 zam1 means to get a shot (an injection). You know 打 from 打鼓 (daa2 gu2) and 打風 (daa2 fung1), and 針 (zam1) means needle. 打針唔痛 = the shot doesn't hurt (you learned 痛 at the doctor's).
  • 咳 kat1 means to cough. When you cough, cover your mouth: 咳嘅時候遮住個口 (kat1 ge3 si4 hau6 ze1 zyu6 go3 hau2).
  • 番梘 faan1 gaan2 means soap. 用番梘洗手 (jung6 faan1 gaan2 sai2 sau2) = wash your hands with soap. 口罩 hau2 zaau3 means face mask: 口 (hau2) is the mouth you learned in body week, and 罩 (zaau3) means a cover.
  • 健康 gin6 hong1 means healthy. 身體健康！ (san1 tai2 gin6 hong1) = good health! People say it as a wish, especially at Chinese New Year. 🧧
