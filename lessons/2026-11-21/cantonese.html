Today, while your other lessons explore teeth and the dentist's office, you'll learn healthy-teeth words: dentist, toothbrush, toothpaste, to brush your teeth, cavity, dental floss, to rinse your mouth and to lose a tooth. You already know 牙 (ngaa4, tooth), 牙齒 (ngaa4 ci2, teeth), 口 (hau2, mouth), 糖 (tong4, sugar) and 醫生 (ji1 sang1, doctor), so you can now talk about taking care of your smile! We use Traditional Chinese characters and Jyutping with tone numbers, the Hong Kong way.

How to read Jyutping (sounds):
  • Letters show the sound. Numbers show the tone (the musical pitch): 1 high, 2 rising, 3 mid, 4 low/falling, 5 low rising, 6 low.
  • Today's words use four of the six tones: 醫 ji1 is tone 1 (high and flat), 口 hau2 is tone 2 (rising), 蛀 zyu3 is tone 3 (middle), and 牙 ngaa4 is tone 4 (low and falling). Listen for a cool trick: 刷 is caat3 in 刷牙 (caat3 ngaa4, to brush teeth), but it changes to a rising caat2 in 牙刷 (ngaa4 caat2, toothbrush). Cantonese speakers often change a tone like this when a word becomes the name of a thing!

Healthy-teeth tips for Ace:
  • Almost every word today has 牙 ngaa4 (tooth) in it! 牙醫 ngaa4 ji1 = dentist: 牙 = tooth + 醫 = doctor / to heal, so a dentist is a "tooth doctor."
  • 牙刷 ngaa4 caat2 = toothbrush ("tooth brush") and 牙膏 ngaa4 gou1 = toothpaste: 膏 means a paste or cream. 牙線 ngaa4 sin3 = dental floss: 線 = thread, so floss is "tooth thread."
  • Flip the order and the meaning changes: 牙刷 (ngaa4 caat2) is the brush, but 刷牙 caat3 ngaa4 ("brush tooth") is the action, to brush your teeth.
  • 蛀牙 zyu3 ngaa4 = a cavity or tooth decay. 蛀 means eaten away by insects or worms, which is why people sometimes talk about "sugar bugs"!
  • 漱口 sau3 hau2 = to rinse your mouth: 漱 = to rinse + 口 = mouth. 甩牙 lat1 ngaa4 = to lose a tooth: 甩 means to come off or fall out. Say 我甩咗牙！ (ngo5 lat1 zo2 ngaa4!) when a baby tooth falls out.
