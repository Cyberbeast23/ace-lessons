🗣️ Cantonese Week 4: School Words (Hong Kong)

Ace, you already know numbers, greetings, family, and food. Today — while your English lessons explore Ancient Egypt — you'll learn everyday school words the Hong Kong way, using Traditional Chinese characters and Jyutping with tone numbers. (A future doctor still needs school!)

How to read Jyutping (sounds):
  • Letters show the sound. Numbers show the tone (the musical pitch): 1 high, 2 rising, 3 mid, 4 low/falling, 5 low rising, 6 low.
  • Example: hok6 haau6 — say hok (tone 6, low) + haau (tone 6, low).
  • Listening/speaking tip: Say each school word out loud three times while looking at the characters. Tap Hear model, then Record and compare!
