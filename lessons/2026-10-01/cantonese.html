🗣️ Cantonese Week 3: Food Words (Hong Kong)

Ace, you already know numbers, greetings, and family words. Today — while your English lessons dig up dinosaurs — you'll learn everyday food words the Hong Kong way, using Traditional Chinese characters and Jyutping with tone numbers. (A palaeontologist still needs lunch!)

How to read Jyutping (sounds):
  • Letters show the sound. Numbers show the tone (the musical pitch): 1 high, 2 rising, 3 mid, 4 low/falling, 5 low rising, 6 low.
  • Example: hou2 sik6 — say hou (tone 2, rising) + sik (tone 6, low).
  • Listening/speaking tip: Say each food word out loud three times while looking at the characters. Tap Hear model, then Record and compare!
