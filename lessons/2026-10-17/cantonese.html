🗣️ Cantonese Week 20: Community, Rules & Voting 投票 (Hong Kong)

Ace, you already know numbers up to 100, plus greetings, family, food, school, body parts, colours, sky & space words, what to say at the doctor's, nature words, question words, music words, trains & travel, brain & sleep words, bees & gardens, electricity & lights, land, people & respect, and ice, snow & giant animals. Today, while your other lessons explore how Canada's government works, you'll learn words for community, rules, and voting: country, government, to vote, to choose, rules, community, to help, and fair. Bonus: an election is 選舉 (syun2 geoi2)! We use Traditional Chinese characters and Jyutping with tone numbers, the Hong Kong way.

How to read Jyutping (sounds):
  • Letters show the sound. Numbers show the tone (the musical pitch): 1 high, 2 rising, 3 mid, 4 low/falling, 5 low rising, 6 low.
  • Example: tau4 in 投票 (to vote) — say tau with tone 4 (low and falling), like a ballot dropping down into the ballot box!
  • Listening/speaking tip: Say each word out loud three times while looking at the characters. Tap Hear model, then Record and compare!

Citizenship tips for Ace:
  • 國家 gwok3 gaa1 means country. You learned 加拿大 (gaa1 naa4 daai6, Canada) in trains week: 加拿大係我嘅國家 = Canada is my country!
  • 政府 zing3 fu2 means government. Add 市 (si5, city) in front: 市政府 = city government.
  • 投票 tau4 piu3 means to vote. 投 = to throw or cast, 票 = a ticket or ballot, so voting is "casting a ballot"! An election is 選舉 (syun2 geoi2).
  • 選擇 syun2 zaak6 means to choose (or a choice). Notice 選 (syun2) is also the first sound in 選舉, election: choosing is what elections are for!
  • 規則 kwai1 zak1 means rules. 守規則 (sau2 kwai1 zak1) = to follow the rules.
  • 社區 se5 keoi1 means community, and 幫手 bong1 sau2 means to help. 我嚟幫手！ (ngo5 lei4 bong1 sau2) = I'll help!
  • 公平 gung1 ping4 means fair. Put 唔 (m4, not) in front, like 唔係: 唔公平！ = That's not fair! ⚖️
