Today, while your other lessons explore the science of avalanches, you'll learn snowy mountain and rescue words: avalanche, to ski, snowy mountain, shovel, warning, search and rescue, dog and temperature. You already know 雪 (syut3, snow), 山 (saan1, mountain), 危險 (ngai4 him2, dangerous), 小心 (siu2 sam1, careful) and 救命 (gau3 meng6, help!), so you can now talk about staying safe in the mountains! We use Traditional Chinese characters and Jyutping with tone numbers, the Hong Kong way.

How to read Jyutping (sounds):
  • Letters show the sound. Numbers show the tone (the musical pitch): 1 high, 2 rising, 3 mid, 4 low/falling, 5 low rising, 6 low.
  • Today's words use four of the six tones: 崩 bang1 is tone 1 (high and flat), 狗 gau2 is tone 2 (rising), 救 gau3 is tone 3 (middle), and 滑 waat6 is tone 6 (low and level). Listen carefully: 狗 gau2 (dog) and 救 gau3 (to rescue) sound the same except for the tone! A 搜救狗 (sau1 gau3 gau2) is a search-and-rescue dog.
  • Listening/speaking tip: Say each word out loud three times while looking at the characters. Tap Hear model, then Record and compare!

Mountain tips for Ace:
  • 雪崩 syut3 bang1 = avalanche, word for word "snow collapse." 崩 means to fall apart or collapse, like a weak layer in the snowpack!
  • 滑雪 waat6 syut3 = to ski: 滑 = slippery / to slide + 雪 = snow. 雪山 syut3 saan1 = snowy mountain: 雪 = snow + 山 = mountain.
  • 鏟 caan2 = shovel. The same sound used as a verb means to shovel or scoop, so you can 鏟雪 (caan2 syut3), shovel snow.
  • 警告 ging2 gou3 = warning: 警 = to alert + 告 = to tell. 搜救 sau1 gau3 = search and rescue: 搜 = to search + 救 = to save. You already know 救 from 救命 (help!).
  • 狗 gau2 = dog and 溫度 wan1 dou6 = temperature: 溫 = warm + 度 = degree. Forecasters check the 溫度 of every layer of snow!
