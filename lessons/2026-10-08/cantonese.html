🗣️ Cantonese Week 10: Nature: Mountains & Volcanoes 大自然 (Hong Kong)

Ace, you already know numbers up to 100, plus greetings, family, food, school, body parts, colours, sky & space words, and what to say at the doctor's. Today — while your maths and reading lessons explore volcanoes and earthquakes — you'll learn words for nature: mountains, volcanoes, rocks, the sea, and more, the Hong Kong way, using Traditional Chinese characters and Jyutping with tone numbers. 大自然 daai6 zi6 jin4 means "nature"!

How to read Jyutping (sounds):
  • Letters show the sound. Numbers show the tone (the musical pitch): 1 high, 2 rising, 3 mid, 4 low/falling, 5 low rising, 6 low.
  • Example: saan1 in 山 (mountain) — say saan with tone 1 (high and level), like singing a high note.
  • Listening/speaking tip: Say each word out loud three times while looking at the characters. Tap Hear model, then Record and compare!

Nature tips for Ace:
  • 火山 fo2 saan1 is a word puzzle: 火 (fire) + 山 (mountain) = a "fire mountain" — a volcano!
  • Look at the character 山 — it even looks like three mountain peaks! 🏔️
  • 行山 haang4 saan1 ("walk mountain") means "to go hiking." People in Hong Kong love to 行山 on weekends.
  • Remember 好 hou2 from 好食 (delicious)? Put it in front of a describing word to mean "very": 好熱 (very hot), 好高 hou2 gou1 (very tall).
  • 地 dei6 means "ground" (you know it from 地球, Earth). 地震 = "ground shakes" = earthquake!
