🗣️ Cantonese Week 9: At the Doctor 睇醫生 (Hong Kong)

Ace, you already know numbers up to 100, plus greetings, family, food, school, body parts, colours, and sky & space words. Today — while your maths and reading lessons explore the heart and blood — you'll learn what to say at the doctor's when you're feeling sick, the Hong Kong way, using Traditional Chinese characters and Jyutping with tone numbers. A future doctor needs to understand patients, so these words are super useful!

How to read Jyutping (sounds):
  • Letters show the sound. Numbers show the tone (the musical pitch): 1 high, 2 rising, 3 mid, 4 low/falling, 5 low rising, 6 low.
  • Example: tung3 in 痛 (hurts) — say tung with tone 3 (middle and level).
  • Listening/speaking tip: Say each word out loud three times while looking at the characters. Tap Hear model, then Record and compare!

Doctor's tips for Ace:
  • 睇醫生 tai2 ji1 sang1 means "see a doctor." 睇 tai2 means "look / see."
  • Remember 心 sam1 (heart) from body-parts day? Add 跳 tiu3 (jump) and you get 心跳 — a heartbeat is your heart "jumping"!
  • To say where it hurts, use a body part + 痛: 頭痛 tau4 tung3 (headache), 肚痛 tou5 tung3 (tummy ache).
  • 唔 m4 means "not." 舒服 syu1 fuk6 means "comfortable," so 唔舒服 = "not comfortable" = unwell.
  • 返 faan1 means "back again," so 好返 = "good again" — better!
