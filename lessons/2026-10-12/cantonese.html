🗣️ Cantonese Week 14: Brain, Thinking & Sleep 腦 (Hong Kong)

Ace, you already know numbers up to 100, plus greetings, family, food, school, body parts, colours, sky & space words, what to say at the doctor's, nature words, question words, music words, and trains & travel. Today, while your other lessons explore the brain and nervous system, you'll learn words for thinking, remembering, and sleeping: brain, think, remember, sleep, dream, clever, tired, and awake. You already know 頭 (head) and 眼 (eye) from body-parts day—today we go inside the head! We use Traditional Chinese characters and Jyutping with tone numbers, the Hong Kong way.

How to read Jyutping (sounds):
  • Letters show the sound. Numbers show the tone (the musical pitch): 1 high, 2 rising, 3 mid, 4 low/falling, 5 low rising, 6 low.
  • Example: nou5 in 腦 (brain) — say nou with tone 5 (low rising), like a thoughtful "hmm?" going up a little.
  • Listening/speaking tip: Say each word out loud three times while looking at the characters. Tap Hear model, then Record and compare!

Brain tips for Ace:
  • 腦 nou5 means brain. Your 腦 is busy when you 諗 nam2 (think)!
  • 記得 gei3 dak1 means remember. After you learn a word, say: 我記得！ (I remember!)
  • 瞓覺 fan3 gaau3 means sleep / go to bed. Sleep helps your brain store memories—just like today's science lesson!
  • 夢 mung6 means dream. 發夢 faat3 mung6 = to have a dream.
  • 聰明 cung1 ming4 means clever / smart. 你好聰明！ = You're so clever!
  • 累 leoi6 (tired) and 醒 sing2 (awake) are opposites. After a good 瞓覺, you 醒 and feel ready to learn. 🧠
