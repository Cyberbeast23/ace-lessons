🗣️ Cantonese Week 1: Greetings (Hong Kong)

Ace, you already know numbers 1 to 10 in Cantonese. Today is your first real conversation lesson: how to say hello, goodbye, thank you, please, and sorry — the way people speak in Hong Kong, using Traditional Chinese characters.

How to read Jyutping (sounds):
  • Letters show the sound. Numbers show the tone (the musical pitch): 1 high, 2 rising, 3 mid, 4 low/falling, 5 low rising, 6 low.
  • Example: nei5 hou2 — say nay (tone 5) + hoe (tone 2).
  • Listening/speaking tip: Say each phrase out loud three times while looking at the characters. Record yourself on a phone if you can, then compare to how Dad says it. Tones matter — the same letters with a different tone number can mean something else!

Part 1: Learn Today's Words

Doctor's tip for Ace: 多謝 do1 ze6 is “thanks” for a gift or big favour. 唔該 m4 goi1 is “please / thanks” for a small service (like someone holding a door). Both are polite — future doctors need good manners in every language!
