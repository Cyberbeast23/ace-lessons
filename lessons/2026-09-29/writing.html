Part 1: The Lesson

Cause → Effect Chain 🔗

A cause is why something happens. An effect is what happens as a result. Strong science writing connects them with linking words.

Cause: Ace drinks milk and plays soccer most days.
↓ because / as a result / therefore / this means / so ↓
Effect: His bones get denser and stronger over time.

Useful linking words: because, since, as a result, therefore, so, this means, consequently, due to, leads to, if…then.

Model Cause-and-Effect Paragraph

Topic sentenceEvidence / causeEffect / explanationConclusion

Bones stay strong only when the body gets the right signals and materials. For example, weight-bearing exercise such as running pushes on the skeleton. As a result, bone cells lay down denser tissue, which makes fractures less likely. In addition, calcium from food is the mineral bones need for hardness. Without enough calcium, bones can become brittle and break more easily. Therefore, daily movement and calcium-rich meals are two simple ways to protect the living scaffold inside you.

Notice: Every sentence stays on the same main idea (what keeps bones strong). The linking words as a result, without, therefore show cause and effect.
