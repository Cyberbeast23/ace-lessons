🗣️ Cantonese Week 11: Question Words & Secrets 問問題 (Hong Kong)

Ace, you already know numbers up to 100, plus greetings, family, food, school, body parts, colours, sky & space words, what to say at the doctor's, and nature words. Today — while your maths and reading lessons crack secret codes — you'll learn the question words every detective needs: who, what, where, when, why, and how, plus how to say "secret" and "take a guess!" We use Traditional Chinese characters and Jyutping with tone numbers, the Hong Kong way. 問問題 man6 man6 tai4 means "to ask questions"!

How to read Jyutping (sounds):
  • Letters show the sound. Numbers show the tone (the musical pitch): 1 high, 2 rising, 3 mid, 4 low/falling, 5 low rising, 6 low.
  • Example: dim2 in 點解 (why) — say dim with tone 2 (rising), like asking "Hm?"
  • Listening/speaking tip: Say each word out loud three times while looking at the characters. Tap Hear model, then Record and compare!

Detective tips for Ace:
  • In Cantonese, the question word usually stays in the same spot as the answer would go. 你係邊個？ (You are who?) → 我係 Ace。 (I am Ace.) No need to move words around like in English!
  • 邊 bin1 means "which." 邊個 = "which one (person)" = who, and 邊度 = "which place" = where.
  • 點 dim2 starts two question words: 點解 = why, and 點樣 = how. Listen carefully to the second syllable!
  • Remember 幾多 gei2 do1 (how many) from numbers day? 幾時 gei2 si4 ("how much time") means when.
  • Say 你估吓！ nei5 gu2 haa5! when you want someone to guess your secret code. 🕵️
