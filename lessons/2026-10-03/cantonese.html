🗣️ Cantonese Week 5: Body Parts (Hong Kong)

Ace, you already know numbers, greetings, family, food, and school. Today — while your English lessons explore oceans — you'll learn everyday body parts the Hong Kong way, using Traditional Chinese characters and Jyutping with tone numbers. (A future doctor needs body words!)

How to read Jyutping (sounds):
  • Letters show the sound. Numbers show the tone (the musical pitch): 1 high, 2 rising, 3 mid, 4 low/falling, 5 low rising, 6 low.
  • Example: tau4 — say tau with tone 4 (low/falling).
  • Listening/speaking tip: Say each body word out loud three times while looking at the characters. Tap Hear model, then Record and compare!
