You already know 紙 (zi2, paper), 手 (sau2, hand), 藝術 (ngai6 seot6, art), 漂亮 (piu3 loeng2, beautiful) and 三 (saam1, three), so you can fold and talk about your own paper crane in Cantonese! We use Traditional Chinese characters and Jyutping with tone numbers, the Hong Kong way.

How to read Jyutping (sounds):
  • Letters show the sound. Numbers show the tone (the musical pitch): 1 high, 2 rising, 3 mid, 4 low/falling, 5 low rising, 6 low.
  • Today's words use all six tones! 方 fong1 is tone 1 (high and flat), 紙 zi2 is tone 2 (rising), 摺 zip3 is tone 3 (middle), 形 jing4 is tone 4 (low and falling), 兩 loeng5 is tone 5 (low rising), and 耐 noi6 is tone 6 (low and level). Listen for the difference!
  • Listening/speaking tip: Say each word out loud three times while looking at the characters. Tap Hear model, then Record and compare!

Paper-folding tips for Ace:
  • 摺紙 zip3 zi2 = origami, word for word "fold paper." 摺 zip3 = to fold. Look at the left side of 摺: it's 扌, the "hand" part, because you fold with your hands!
  • 正方形 zing3 fong1 jing4 = square and 三角形 saam1 gok3 jing4 = triangle. 形 jing4 means "shape," so a triangle is a "three-corner shape"! 角 gok3 = corner or angle.
  • 對摺 deoi3 zip3 = fold in half, and 兩半 loeng5 bun3 = two halves. When you 對摺 a 正方形 along the diagonal, you get two 三角形!
  • 對稱 deoi3 cing3 = symmetrical: both halves match. Notice 對 deoi3 again, as in 對摺. It means "facing each other" or "a matching pair."
  • 耐心 noi6 sam1 = patience. 心 sam1 = heart, so patience is a heart that lasts a long time. 作品 zok3 ban2 = a work of art or something you created: 我嘅作品 = my creation.
