🗣️ Cantonese Week 7: Sky & Space 天空同太空 (Hong Kong)

Ace, you already know numbers, greetings, family, food, school, body parts, and colours. Today — while your English lessons blast off into space — you'll learn words for the sky and space the Hong Kong way, using Traditional Chinese characters and Jyutping with tone numbers. Some example sentences reuse words you already know, like 藍色 laam4 sik1 and 好 hou2!

How to read Jyutping (sounds):
  • Letters show the sound. Numbers show the tone (the musical pitch): 1 high, 2 rising, 3 mid, 4 low/falling, 5 low rising, 6 low.
  • Example: kau4 in 地球 — say kau with tone 4 (low/falling).
  • Listening/speaking tip: Say each space word out loud three times while looking at the characters. Tap Hear model, then Record and compare!
