🚀 Math Week 8: Space & Solar System Math Lab

Mission control to Ace! Today you'll level up with integers (negative numbers), powers of ten and scientific notation, circle-graph angles, a missing value from a mean, percent decrease, exponents, two-step equations, ratios, and huge-number rates — the same maths that space agencies use to plan rockets, orbits, and spacewalks.

Part 1: Learn the Moves

The 4-Step Problem Solver (your pre-launch checklist):
  1. Read: What is the question actually asking? Underline the numbers and units.
  2. Plan: Which operations do I need, and in what order?
  3. Solve: Do one step at a time and write each step down.
  4. Check: Is my answer reasonable? Did I answer the question that was asked?

Worked Example 1: Temperatures below zero (integers)

A crater on the Moon is about 100 °C in sunlight and about −150 °C in shadow. What is the difference?

Step 1: Difference = warmer − colder = 100 − (−150).

Step 2: Subtracting a negative is the same as adding: 100 + 150 = 250 degrees.

Check on a number line: from −150 up to 0 is 150, and from 0 up to 100 is 100. 150 + 100 = 250 ✔

Worked Example 2: Powers of ten and scientific notation

Jupiter is about 780 000 000 km from the Sun. Write that in scientific notation.

Step 1: Put the decimal after the first digit: 7.8.

Step 2: Count how many places the decimal moved: 8 places. So 780 000 000 = 7.8 × 108 km.

Going backwards: 2.5 × 104 means move the decimal 4 places right: 25 000.

Worked Example 3: Circle-graph angles

A whole circle is 360°. In a survey, 20% of students chose Jupiter as their favourite planet. How big is Jupiter's slice?

20% of 360° = 0.20 × 360 = 72°.

Worked Example 4: The missing value from a mean

Three rocket tests lasted 10, 14, and x seconds. The mean was 12 s. Find x.

Step 1: Total = mean × number of values = 12 × 3 = 36.

Step 2: x = 36 − 10 − 14 = 12 s.

Worked Example 5: Percent decrease and exponents

A fuel tank held 200 L and now holds 150 L. Decrease = 50 L. Percent decrease = 50 ÷ 200 × 100 = 25%. (Always divide by the starting amount.)

Exponents (BEDMAS): 3² × 4 − 2³ → 9 × 4 = 36; 2³ = 8; 36 − 8 = 28.
