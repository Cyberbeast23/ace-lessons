Today, while your other lessons follow paramedics and the ER, you'll learn emergency and hospital words: hospital, ambulance, nurse, to breathe, emergency, help!, to get hurt, and to make a phone call. You already know 醫生 (ji1 sang1, doctor), 痛 (tung3, sore), 心跳 (sam1 tiu3, heartbeat), 幫手 (bong1 sau2, to help) and 危險 (ngai4 him2, dangerous), so listen for them in today's sentences. We use Traditional Chinese characters and Jyutping with tone numbers, the Hong Kong way.

How to read Jyutping (sounds):
  • Letters show the sound. Numbers show the tone (the musical pitch): 1 high, 2 rising, 3 mid, 4 low/falling, 5 low rising, 6 low.
  • Example: 醫院 ji1 jyun2 (hospital) starts with the same 醫 ji1 as 醫生 (doctor), tone 1, high and flat. Then 院 jyun2 is tone 2, rising like a question. Compare 救命 gau3 meng6: tone 3 is mid and level, then tone 6 is low and level. And 打電話 daa2 din6 waa2 has two rising tone 2s, on the first and last syllables!
  • Listening/speaking tip: Say each word out loud three times while looking at the characters. Tap Hear model, then Record and compare!

Emergency tips for Ace:
  • 醫院 ji1 jyun2 = hospital: 醫 = to heal (like in 醫生, doctor) + 院 = a big building or institution. A hospital is a "healing building."
  • 白車 baak6 ce1 = ambulance. In Hong Kong, ambulances are white, so people just call them "white cars"! You know 白色 (white) and 火車 (train), so you already know both characters. The formal word is 救護車 (gau3 wu6 ce1).
  • 護士 wu6 si6 = nurse. 護 means to protect or care for, the same 護 as in 救護車.
  • 呼吸 fu1 kap1 = to breathe. 呼 = breathe out, 吸 = breathe in. Paramedics check 呼吸 and 心跳 first!
  • 緊急 gan2 gap1 = emergency, urgent. In Hong Kong, the emergency number is 999 (gau2 gau2 gau2). In Canada, we call 911.
  • 救命 gau3 meng6 = to save a life; shouted on its own, 救命呀！ means "Help!" 救 = to rescue, 命 = life.
  • 受傷 sau6 soeng1 = to get hurt or injured. 打電話 daa2 din6 waa2 = to make a phone call (literally "hit the electric speech"!). A safety sentence: 有人受傷，快啲打電話！ (jau5 jan4 sau6 soeng1, faai3 di1 daa2 din6 waa2!) = Someone is hurt, make a phone call quickly! 📞
