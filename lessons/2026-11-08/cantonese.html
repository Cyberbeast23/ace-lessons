Today, while your other lessons explore the Vikings and their voyages to North America, you'll learn words for explorers and adventures: Vikings, explorer, brave, island, to row a boat, to discover, shield and legend. You already know 船 (syun4, boat), 海 (hoi2, sea), 地圖 (dei6 tou4, map), 風 (fung1, wind) and 凍 (dung3, cold), so you can tell a whole Viking story! We use Traditional Chinese characters and Jyutping with tone numbers, the Hong Kong way.

How to read Jyutping (sounds):
  • Letters show the sound. Numbers show the tone (the musical pitch): 1 high, 2 rising, 3 mid, 4 low/falling, 5 low rising, 6 low.
  • Example: 島 dou2 (island) is tone 2, rising like a question, but 划 waa4 (to row) is tone 4, low and falling. Listen for the difference!
  • Listening/speaking tip: Say each word out loud three times while looking at the characters. Tap Hear model, then Record and compare!

Explorer tips for Ace:
  • 維京人 wai4 ging1 jan4 = Vikings / Norse people. 人 (jan4) = person — you already know it!
  • 探險家 taam3 him2 gaa1 = explorer. 家 (gaa1) at the end often means "an expert" — like 科學家 (fo1 hok6 gaa1, scientist).
  • 勇敢 jung5 gam2 = brave. Say 好勇敢！ (hou2 jung5 gam2) = so brave!
  • 島 dou2 = island. 冰島 (bing1 dou2) = Iceland — literally "ice island"!
  • 划船 waa4 syun4 = to row a boat. 發現 faat3 jin6 = to discover. 盾牌 teon5 paai4 = shield (Viking shields were round!). 傳說 cyun4 syut3 = legend.
