Ace, you already know numbers up to 100, plus greetings, family, food, school, body parts, colours, sky & space words, what to say at the doctor's, nature words, question words, music words, trains & travel, brain & sleep words, bees & gardens, electricity & lights, land, people & respect, ice, snow & giant animals, community, rules & voting, weather & storms, germ-fighter words, ancient China words, flight words, gold rush words, kitchen & flavour words, and cartoon & movie words. Today, while your other lessons explore the Burgess Shale, you'll learn fossil and sea-creature words: fossil, animal, ocean, shrimp, strange, museum, hard, and to swim. You already know 海 (hoi2, sea), 石頭 (sek6 tau4, rock), 好耐以前 (hou2 noi6 ji5 cin4, a long time ago) and 睇 (tai2, to look), so listen for them in today's sentences. We use Traditional Chinese characters and Jyutping with tone numbers, the Hong Kong way.

How to read Jyutping (sounds):
  • Letters show the sound. Numbers show the tone (the musical pitch): 1 high, 2 rising, 3 mid, 4 low/falling, 5 low rising, 6 low.
  • Example: 化石 faa3 sek6 (fossil) uses the same 石 sek6 as 石頭 (rock), tone 6, low and level. Compare 蝦 haa1, tone 1, high and flat, and 奇怪 kei4 gwaai3: tone 4 starts low and falls even lower, then tone 3 is mid and level. And 硬 ngaang6 starts with the "ng" sound from the end of "sing"!
  • Listening/speaking tip: Say each word out loud three times while looking at the characters. Tap Hear model, then Record and compare!

Fossil tips for Ace:
  • 化石 faa3 sek6 = fossil: 化 = to change, 石 = stone. A fossil is something that "changed into stone," exactly what you read about today!
  • 動物 dung6 mat6 = animal: 動 = moving (you met it in 動畫 yesterday!) + 物 = thing. An animal is a "moving thing."
  • 海洋 hoi2 joeng4 = ocean. You know 海 (hoi2, sea); 洋 makes it the big, wide ocean. 太平洋 (taai3 ping4 joeng4) is the Pacific Ocean, right next to BC.
  • 蝦 haa1 = shrimp or prawn. Anomalocaris means "unusual shrimp," so you could call it 奇怪嘅蝦 (kei4 gwaai3 ge3 haa1)! And yes, 蝦餃 (haa1 gaau2) at dim sum is a shrimp dumpling. 🦐
  • 奇怪 kei4 gwaai3 = strange or weird. 好奇怪呀！ = So weird!
  • 博物館 bok3 mat6 gun2 = museum. It has three syllables, so say it slowly: bok3 · mat6 · gun2.
  • 硬 ngaang6 = hard (the opposite of soft). 游水 jau4 seoi2 = to swim (literally "move through water," and you know 水!). A fossil fan's sentence: 好耐以前，好多奇怪嘅動物喺海洋度游水。 (hou2 noi6 ji5 cin4, hou2 do1 kei4 gwaai3 ge3 dung6 mat6 hai2 hoi2 joeng4 dou6 jau4 seoi2.) = A long time ago, lots of strange animals swam in the ocean. 🌊
