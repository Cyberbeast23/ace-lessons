Today, while your other lessons explore firefighters and fire safety, you'll learn words for staying safe in a fire: firefighter, fire engine, smoke, safe, be careful, exit, ladder and fire extinguisher. You already know 火 (fo2, fire), 危險 (ngai4 him2, dangerous), 救命 (gau3 meng6, help!), 打電話 (daa2 din6 waa2, make a phone call) and 勇敢 (jung5 gam2, brave), so you can describe a whole fire rescue! We use Traditional Chinese characters and Jyutping with tone numbers, the Hong Kong way.

How to read Jyutping (sounds):
  • Letters show the sound. Numbers show the tone (the musical pitch): 1 high, 2 rising, 3 mid, 4 low/falling, 5 low rising, 6 low.
  • Example: 煙 jin1 (smoke) is tone 1, high and flat, but 員 jyun4 (in 消防員) is tone 4, low and falling. Listen for the difference!
  • Listening/speaking tip: Say each word out loud three times while looking at the characters. Tap Hear model, then Record and compare!

Fire-safety tips for Ace:
  • 消防 siu1 fong4 means "fire-fighting / fire protection." Add 員 (jyun4, a staff member) → 消防員 = firefighter. Add 車 (ce1, vehicle) → 消防車 = fire engine!
  • You already know 運動員 (wan6 dung6 jyun4, athlete) — same 員 ending for a person who does a job.
  • 煙 jin1 = smoke. 好多煙！ (hou2 do1 jin1) = So much smoke!
  • 小心 siu2 sam1 = be careful (literally "small heart"). 小心火！ = Careful of the fire!
  • 安全 on1 cyun4 = safe. 出口 ceot1 hau2 = exit (literally "out mouth" — look for it on signs!). 梯 tai1 = ladder. 滅火筒 mit6 fo2 tung2 = fire extinguisher (literally "put-out-fire tube").
