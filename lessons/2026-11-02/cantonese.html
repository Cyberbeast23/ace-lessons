Today, while your other lessons explore photosynthesis and BC's giant rainforest trees, you'll learn words for plants and forests: leaf, root, seed, sunlight, air, forest, to plant, and to water the plants. You already know 樹 (syu6, tree) and 水 (seoi2, water) from nature and food days, and 花 (faa1, flower) from bee day, so listen for them hiding inside today's words. We use Traditional Chinese characters and Jyutping with tone numbers, the Hong Kong way.

How to read Jyutping (sounds):
  • Letters show the sound. Numbers show the tone (the musical pitch): 1 high, 2 rising, 3 mid, 4 low/falling, 5 low rising, 6 low.
  • Example: 種 zung3 (to plant) is tone 3, middle and flat, but in 種子 zung2 zi2 (seed) the same character is read with tone 2, rising. One character, two tones, two jobs!
  • Listening/speaking tip: Say each word out loud three times while looking at the characters. Tap Hear model, then Record and compare!

Plant tips for Ace:
  • 樹葉 syu6 jip6 = leaf, literally "tree leaf." You know 樹 (tree) already! The character 葉 has 艹 (grass) on top, the sign for plants. Look for 艹 in 花 (flower) too.
  • 根 gan1 = root. It has 木 (wood/tree) on the left, because roots belong to trees. 樹根 = tree root.
  • 種子 zung2 zi2 = seed, and 種 zung3 = to plant. 種樹 (zung3 syu6) = plant a tree, 種花 (zung3 faa1) = plant flowers.
  • 陽光 joeng4 gwong1 = sunlight and 空氣 hung1 hei3 = air. Plants need both to do photosynthesis!
  • 森林 sam1 lam4 = forest. Count the trees: 林 has two 木 (a grove), and 森 has three 木 (a thick forest)! 淋水 lam4 seoi2 = water the plants (淋 = pour or sprinkle).
