🗣️ Cantonese Week 2: Family Members (Hong Kong)

Ace, you already know numbers 1–10 and greetings like 你好 and 多謝. Today you'll learn how to name family members the Hong Kong way, using Traditional Chinese characters and Jyutping with tone numbers.

How to read Jyutping (sounds):
  • Letters show the sound. Numbers show the tone (the musical pitch): 1 high, 2 rising, 3 mid, 4 low/falling, 5 low rising, 6 low.
  • Example: baa4 baa1 — say bah (tone 4) + bah (tone 1, higher).
  • Listening/speaking tip: Say each family word out loud three times while looking at the characters. Many family words repeat the same syllable twice (爸爸, 媽媽, 哥哥) — that doubling is a warm, familiar sound in Cantonese. Record yourself if you can, then compare with Dad!

Part 1: Learn Today's Words

Doctor's tip for Ace: In Hong Kong Cantonese, 爺爺 ye4 ye2 is Dad's dad (paternal grandpa), and 嫲嫲 maa4 maa4 is Dad's mum (paternal grandma). Mum's parents use different words (公公 / 婆婆) — you'll learn those another day. Future doctors meet whole families, so knowing the right title shows respect!
