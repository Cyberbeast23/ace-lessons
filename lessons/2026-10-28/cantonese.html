Today, while your other lessons explore how robots sense, think and move, you'll learn robot words: robot, program, battery, motor, gear, press the button, move, and stop. You already know 電 (din6, electricity), 開 (hoi1, switch on) and 好快 (hou2 faai3, very fast), so listen for them in today's sentences. We use Traditional Chinese characters and Jyutping with tone numbers, the Hong Kong way.

How to read Jyutping (sounds):
  • Letters show the sound. Numbers show the tone (the musical pitch): 1 high, 2 rising, 3 mid, 4 low/falling, 5 low rising, 6 low.
  • Example: 機械人 gei1 haai6 jan4 (robot) climbs down the tone ladder: high flat (tone 1), then low level (tone 6), then lowest falling (tone 4). 郁 juk1 (move) is tone 1 but short and clipped because it ends in k. 停 ting4 (stop) is tone 4, low and falling, like a robot powering down.
  • Listening/speaking tip: Say each word out loud three times while looking at the characters. Tap Hear model, then Record and compare!

Robot tips for Ace:
  • 機械人 gei1 haai6 jan4 = robot: 機械 = machine, machinery + 人 = person. A robot is a "machine person"! (You may also hear 機器人 gei1 hei3 jan4; both are used.)
  • 電池 din6 ci4 = battery: 電 = electricity (you learned it on electricity day!) + 池 = pool. A battery is an "electricity pool." When it's empty, say 冇電 mou5 din6, "no power."
  • 摩打 mo1 daa2 = motor. It is a sound loan from English: say "motor" quickly and it sounds like mo-daa! Hong Kong Cantonese has lots of these, like 巴士 (baa1 si2, bus).
  • 齒輪 ci2 leon4 = gear: 齒 = tooth + 輪 = wheel. A gear really is a "tooth wheel," just like the gears in your math lesson!
  • 按掣 on3 zai3 = press the button: 按 = to press + 掣 = switch or button.
  • A robot command: 郁！停！ (juk1! ting4!) = Move! Stop! Try it with a friend playing the robot. 🤖
