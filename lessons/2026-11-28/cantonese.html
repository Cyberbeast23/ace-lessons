Today, while your other lessons follow chocolate from the cacao bean to the bar, you'll learn chocolate and sweet-treat words: chocolate, bitter, milk, to melt, flavour, fragrant, dessert and to share. You already know 甜 (tim4, sweet), 酸 (syun1, sour), 鹹 (haam4, salty), 糖 (tong4, sugar) and 好味 (hou2 mei6, tasty), so now you can describe every flavour of a chocolate bar! We use Traditional Chinese characters and Jyutping with tone numbers, the Hong Kong way.

How to read Jyutping (sounds):
  • Letters show the sound. Numbers show the tone (the musical pitch): 1 high, 2 rising, 3 mid, 4 low/falling, 5 low rising, 6 low.
  • Today's words use all six tones! 香 hoeng1 is tone 1 (high and flat), 苦 fu2 is tone 2 (rising), 化 faa3 is tone 3 (middle and flat), 牛 ngau4 is tone 4 (low and falling), 奶 naai5 is tone 5 (low rising), and 味 mei6 is tone 6 (low and flat). Listen carefully to 香 hoeng1 (fragrant) and 享 hoeng2 in 分享 (to share): same sound, different tone, totally different meaning!
  • Listening/speaking tip: Say each word out loud three times while looking at the characters. Tap Hear model, then Record and compare!

Chocolatier tips for Ace:
  • 朱古力 zyu1 gu1 lik1 = chocolate. It's a sound loan: say it fast and it sounds like "choc-o-late"! (In Mandarin it's 巧克力 qiǎokèlì, but in Hong Kong everyone says 朱古力.) 牛奶朱古力 = milk chocolate, 黑朱古力 hak1 zyu1 gu1 lik1 = dark chocolate.
  • 苦 fu2 = bitter. Now you know all the tastes: 甜 sweet, 酸 sour, 鹹 salty and 苦 bitter. Cacao beans straight from the pod are very 苦!
  • 牛奶 ngau4 naai5 = milk: 牛 (cow) + 奶 (milk), "cow's milk." 融化 jung4 faa3 = to melt. You'll hear it when ice cream or chocolate melts in the sun.
  • 味道 mei6 dou6 = flavour, taste. It shares 味 with 好味 (hou2 mei6, tasty). 香 hoeng1 = fragrant, smells good. Fun fact: 香港 hoeng1 gong2, Hong Kong, means "fragrant harbour"!
  • 甜品 tim4 ban2 = dessert: 甜 (sweet) + 品 (item, product), "sweet things." 分享 fan1 hoeng2 = to share: 分 (to divide) + 享 (to enjoy), "divide and enjoy." Sharing chocolate is the best way to eat it!
