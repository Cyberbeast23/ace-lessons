🗣️ Cantonese Week 15: Bees, Flowers & Gardens 蜜蜂 (Hong Kong)

Ace, you already know numbers up to 100, plus greetings, family, food, school, body parts, colours, sky & space words, what to say at the doctor's, nature words, question words, music words, trains & travel, and brain & sleep words. Today, while your other lessons explore honeybees and pollination, you'll learn words for bees, flowers, honey, and busy garden life: honeybee, flower, fly, sweet, honey, insect, garden, and busy. You already know 樹 (tree) and 山 (mountain) from nature day—today we zoom in on the little buzzing helpers! We use Traditional Chinese characters and Jyutping with tone numbers, the Hong Kong way.

How to read Jyutping (sounds):
  • Letters show the sound. Numbers show the tone (the musical pitch): 1 high, 2 rising, 3 mid, 4 low/falling, 5 low rising, 6 low.
  • Example: mat6 fung1 in 蜜蜂 (honeybee) — say mat with tone 6 (low level), then fung with tone 1 (high and level), like a steady buzz!
  • Listening/speaking tip: Say each word out loud three times while looking at the characters. Tap Hear model, then Record and compare!

Bee tips for Ace:
  • 蜜蜂 mat6 fung1 means honeybee. 蜜 = honey/sweet, 蜂 = bee. Together: the honey bee!
  • 花 faa1 means flower. Bees 飛 fei1 (fly) from 花 to 花.
  • 蜂蜜 fung1 mat6 means honey (bee + honey/sweet). Notice the order flips from 蜜蜂!
  • 甜 tim4 means sweet. Honey tastes 甜!
  • 蟲 cung4 means insect / bug. A bee is a kind of 蟲.
  • 花園 faa1 jyun4 means garden (flower + garden/park). Bees are 忙 mong4 (busy) in the 花園! 🐝
