Ace, you already know numbers up to 100, plus greetings, family, food, school, body parts, colours, sky & space words, what to say at the doctor's, nature words, question words, music words, trains & travel, brain & sleep words, bees & gardens, electricity & lights, land, people & respect, ice, snow & giant animals, community, rules & voting, weather & storms, germ-fighter words, and ancient China words. Today, while your other lessons explore how airplanes fly, you'll learn flight words: airplane, airport, pilot, bird, wing, to take off, very fast, and very high. You already know 飛 (fei1, to fly) and 天空 (tin1 hung1, sky), so listen for them inside today's words! Bonus: to land is 降落 (gong3 lok6)! We use Traditional Chinese characters and Jyutping with tone numbers, the Hong Kong way.

How to read Jyutping (sounds):
  • Letters show the sound. Numbers show the tone (the musical pitch): 1 high, 2 rising, 3 mid, 4 low/falling, 5 low rising, 6 low.
  • Example: fei1 gei1 in 飛機 (airplane) — both syllables are tone 1 (high and flat). Keep your voice up high and steady, like a plane cruising level: "feiii geiii."
  • Listening/speaking tip: Say each word out loud three times while looking at the characters. Tap Hear model, then Record and compare!

Flight tips for Ace:
  • 飛機 fei1 gei1 means airplane. 飛 (fei1) = fly and 機 (gei1) = machine, so an airplane is a "flying machine," exactly what the Wright brothers called theirs!
  • 機場 gei1 coeng4 means airport (a "machine field"), and 機師 gei1 si1 means pilot (a "machine master"). You met 師 before in 老師 (lou5 si1), teacher. To fly a plane is 開飛機 (hoi1 fei1 gei1), literally "drive the airplane."
  • 雀仔 zoek3 zai2 means bird. 仔 (zai2) often means "little," so it is a "little bird." 雀仔喺天空飛 = The bird flies in the sky.
  • 翼 jik6 means wing. Birds and planes both have 翼! Use the measure word 隻 (zek3): 兩隻翼 (loeng5 zek3 jik6) = two wings.
  • 起飛 hei2 fei1 means to take off. 起 (hei2) = rise up. Its opposite is 降落 (gong3 lok6), to land. 飛機起飛喇！ = The plane is taking off!
  • 好快 hou2 faai3 = very fast and 好高 hou2 gou1 = very high (or very tall). To say how something flies, add 飛得 (fei1 dak1): 飛得好快 = flies very fast, 飛得好高 = flies very high. ✈️
