🗣️ Cantonese Week 19: Ice, Snow & Giant Animals 冰 (Hong Kong)

Ace, you already know numbers up to 100, plus greetings, family, food, school, body parts, colours, sky & space words, what to say at the doctor's, nature words, question words, music words, trains & travel, brain & sleep words, bees & gardens, electricity & lights, and land, people & respect. Today, while your other lessons explore the Ice Age, you'll learn words for ice, snow, cold, and giant animals: ice, snow, cold, fur, elephant, tooth/tusk, very big, and a long time ago. Bonus: the Cantonese name for a woolly mammoth is 長毛象 (coeng4 mou4 zoeng6), "long-fur elephant"! We use Traditional Chinese characters and Jyutping with tone numbers, the Hong Kong way.

How to read Jyutping (sounds):
  • Letters show the sound. Numbers show the tone (the musical pitch): 1 high, 2 rising, 3 mid, 4 low/falling, 5 low rising, 6 low.
  • Example: bing1 in 冰 (ice) — say bing with tone 1 (high and level), long and flat like a frozen sheet of ice!
  • Listening/speaking tip: Say each word out loud three times while looking at the characters. Tap Hear model, then Record and compare!

Ice Age tips for Ace:
  • 冰 bing1 means ice and 雪 syut3 means snow. 落雪 (lok6 syut3, "fall snow") = it's snowing!
  • 凍 dung3 means cold. Put 好 (very) in front, just like 好熱 (very hot) from nature week: 好凍 = very cold!
  • 毛 mou4 means fur or hair, and 大象 daai6 zoeng6 means elephant (大 = big, 象 = elephant). Put them together: 長毛象 = long-fur elephant = woolly mammoth! 🦣
  • 牙 ngaa4 means tooth. A mammoth's tusk is a giant tooth, so 牙 works for tusk too.
  • 好大 hou2 daai6 means very big. Compare 好遠 (very far) from trains week: 好 + describing word.
  • 好耐以前 hou2 noi6 ji5 cin4 means a long time ago (耐 = a long time, 以前 = before). Great for starting a story about the Ice Age! 🧊
