🗣️ Cantonese Week 16: Electricity & Lights 電 (Hong Kong)

Ace, you already know numbers up to 100, plus greetings, family, food, school, body parts, colours, sky & space words, what to say at the doctor's, nature words, question words, music words, trains & travel, brain & sleep words, and bees & gardens. Today, while your other lessons explore electricity and circuits, you'll learn words for electricity, lights, and staying safe: electricity, lamp/light, turn on, turn off, bright, dark, wire, and danger. We use Traditional Chinese characters and Jyutping with tone numbers, the Hong Kong way.

How to read Jyutping (sounds):
  • Letters show the sound. Numbers show the tone (the musical pitch): 1 high, 2 rising, 3 mid, 4 low/falling, 5 low rising, 6 low.
  • Example: din6 in 電 (electricity) — say din with tone 6 (low and level), like a steady hum of power!
  • Listening/speaking tip: Say each word out loud three times while looking at the characters. Tap Hear model, then Record and compare!

Electricity tips for Ace:
  • 電 din6 means electricity. You'll hear it in 電話 (telephone) and 電腦 (computer) too!
  • 燈 dang1 means lamp / light. 開燈 = turn on the light; 關燈 = turn off the light.
  • 開 hoi1 means turn on / open. 關 gwaan1 means turn off / close.
  • 亮 loeng6 means bright. 暗 am3 means dark.
  • 線 sin3 means wire / line. Power travels along the 線—but never touch a broken one!
  • 危險 ngai4 him2 means danger / dangerous. Electricity can be 危險—stay safe! ⚡
