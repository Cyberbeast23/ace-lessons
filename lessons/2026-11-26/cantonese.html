You already know 細菌 (sai3 kwan2, bacteria) and 森林 (sam1 lam4, forest), so now you can talk about what grows on the forest floor! We use Traditional Chinese characters and Jyutping with tone numbers, the Hong Kong way.

How to read Jyutping (sounds):
  • Letters show the sound. Numbers show the tone (the musical pitch): 1 high, 2 rising, 3 mid, 4 low/falling, 5 low rising, 6 low.
  • Today's words use all six tones! 菇 gu1 is tone 1 (high and flat), 菌 kwan2 is tone 2 (rising), 發 faat3 is tone 3 (middle and flat), 蘑 mo4 is tone 4 (low and falling), 有 jau5 is tone 5 (low rising), and 毒 duk6 is tone 6 (low and flat). Listen for a pair that sounds the same: 孢子 baau1 zi2 (spore) and 麵包 min6 baau1 (bread) both have the sound baau1. Look closely: the character 孢 even has 包 hiding inside it!
  • Listening/speaking tip: Say each word out loud three times while looking at the characters. Tap Hear model, then Record and compare!

Fungus tips for Ace:
  • 蘑菇 mo4 gu1 = mushroom. Both characters have the grass radical 艹 on top, a clue that they grow from the ground. In Hong Kong you'll also hear just 菇 (gu1), as in 冬菇 (dung1 gu1), the dried black mushrooms in soup!
  • 真菌 zan1 kwan2 = fungus: 真 (true, real) + 菌 (germ, fungus). You already know 細菌 sai3 kwan2 (bacteria): 細 means "tiny." Both words share 菌!
  • 孢子 baau1 zi2 = spore. 子 (zi2) often means "seed" or "small thing," just like in 種子 (zung2 zi2, seed).
  • 有毒 jau5 duk6 = poisonous: 有 (to have) + 毒 (poison), "has poison." The safety rule: 野生蘑菇可能有毒！ (je5 sang1 mo4 gu1 ho2 nang4 jau5 duk6! Wild mushrooms might be poisonous!)
  • 發霉 faat3 mui4 = to go mouldy: 發 (to grow, to break out) + 霉 (mould). 腐爛 fu6 laan6 = to rot. 酵母 haau1 mou5 = yeast: 酵 (ferment) + 母 (mother), the "mother of fermenting" that makes 麵包 min6 baau1 (bread) rise.
