🗣️ Cantonese Week 12: Music 音樂 (Hong Kong)

Ace, you already know numbers up to 100, plus greetings, family, food, school, body parts, colours, sky & space words, what to say at the doctor's, nature words, and question words. Today — while your maths and reading lessons explore the science of sound — you'll learn music words: how to say music, sing, dance, listen, "sounds nice," piano, guitar, and playing the drums. We use Traditional Chinese characters and Jyutping with tone numbers, the Hong Kong way. Fun fact: Cantonese is a tonal language, so speaking it is a little like singing — the pitch of your voice changes the meaning!

How to read Jyutping (sounds):
  • Letters show the sound. Numbers show the tone (the musical pitch): 1 high, 2 rising, 3 mid, 4 low/falling, 5 low rising, 6 low.
  • Example: teng1 in 聽 (listen) — say teng with tone 1 (high and level), like holding a high note on a flute.
  • Listening/speaking tip: Say each word out loud three times while looking at the characters. Tap Hear model, then Record and compare!

Music tips for Ace:
  • 好聽 hou2 teng1 is literally "good to listen to." Remember 好食 hou2 sik6 (tasty) from food day? Same pattern: 好 + a verb!
  • 唱歌 coeng3 go1 is literally "sing song," and 跳舞 tiu3 mou5 is "jump dance."
  • For instruments with keys or strings, use 彈 taan4 (play): 彈鋼琴 = play the piano, 彈結他 = play the guitar. For drums, use 打 daa2 (hit): 打鼓 = play the drums.
  • 結他 git3 taa1 sounds like the English word "guitar" — Cantonese borrowed it!
  • Say 你聽吓！ nei5 teng1 haa5! when you want someone to listen to your favourite song. 🎶
